Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Timeline Tracker ("the app") is a personal project built to help users track and compare important dates and milestones. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Guest mode: No personal information is collected or stored. Any events you create exist only temporarily in your browser session and are not saved to any server.
  • Registered accounts: If you sign up, we collect the email address you provide (and, if you use "Sign in with Google," the basic profile information Google shares — typically your name and email).
  • Event data: Names and dates of events you create in the app (e.g., "EOI," "Assessment"), so the app can display and compare them for you.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information is used solely to provide the app's core functionality: storing your events so you can access them again later, and authenticating you when you log in. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Registered user data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and authentication provider. Supabase's own privacy and security practices apply to data stored on their infrastructure. You can review Supabase's privacy policy at supabase.com/privacy.

4. Third-Party Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google's own privacy policy governs the information they share with us during that process. We only request basic profile information (name and email) — we do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, or other Google services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Guest session data is never persisted and is cleared automatically after a period of inactivity or when you close your browser. Registered account data is retained until you delete your account or request deletion. To request deletion of your data, contact us using the information below.

6. Cookies and Tracking

The app uses only the minimal browser storage required to keep you signed in (via Supabase's authentication session). We do not use third-party advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

This is an independently developed personal project. For questions about this policy or your data, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
